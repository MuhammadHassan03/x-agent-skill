# Roadmap

Owner: Muhammad Hassan. Written from the owner's own plan; nothing here is a commitment to a date.

## Principle

Everything in this repo stays MIT and free forever, including every honesty and safety feature: the
humanizer, the linters, the fine print and the algorithm tracker. Paid things are additive: data,
services, or a hosted product. Nothing paid ever posts to X or touches X.

## 1. Algorithm briefing (free)

`tools/algo_watch.py` already produces dated weight diffs from xai-org/x-algorithm. They become a
free weekly email: what changed in X's ranking code, and what to do about it.

- Starts after the 2026-11-19 results post.
- Quiet-week rule: when a week has zero weight changes, the email is one line and carries no analysis.
- Nothing is sold with it. No paid tier, no JSON feed, until there are subscribers to ask.

## 2. voice.md setup, done for the customer (first paid test)

The owner writes a customer's voice.md for them, by hand. No infrastructure, no hosted product. It is
the first test of whether anyone pays.

It starts only when both are true:
- the 30-day results post is out, and
- ten people have asked for help setting up their voice.md.

If the second condition is not met, nothing is sold and nothing else on this page changes.
